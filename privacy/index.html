How we protect your data

2. What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we receive the following, all of which you type or select yourself:

  • Project type and the services you are interested in
  • Project location, approximate size, budget band and timeline
  • Your name and email address, and your phone number if you give one
  • The message you write, and the consent you tick
  • A one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself), used only to limit abuse. It is not used to identify you.

3. What we do not collect

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics, advertising tools, embedded maps or social-media widgets. Fonts, images and videos are served from this site only, so your browser makes no requests to other companies when you visit. Contact buttons for WhatsApp or email only open those services when you click them, and then their own policies apply.

4. How we use your data

We use your data only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Advise on our services and discuss a possible project
  • Keep a record of the enquiry so that none is lost

5. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the consent box and submitting the form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

6. How the form works and where data is stored

When you press send, your enquiry is transmitted to our web server over an encrypted connection. The server does two things: it emails the enquiry to the studio mailbox, and it appends it to an access-protected enquiry log kept on the same web server, so that no enquiry is lost if an email does not arrive. The log is not accessible from the web.

7. Retention

If no business relationship is established, we delete your enquiry, from the log and from the mailbox, within 12 months of receiving it. If we start working together we retain the information for the duration of the relationship and any subsequent legal retention period. Anti-abuse records (hashed IP and timestamps) are deleted automatically within one hour.

8. Sharing

We do not sell, trade or share your personal data. Our web hosting provider and our email provider process data on our behalf as processors, solely to deliver the website and the email. Your data is not used for any other purpose.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access-restricted storage and spam protection that does not rely on tracking.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: limit how we use your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email klucas.lucas@gmail.com.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your data appropriately, you can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, Bezuidenhoutseweg 30, 2594 AV Den Haag, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new revision date.
