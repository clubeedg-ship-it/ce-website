From idea to space

Sketch, visualisation, realisation

Every project starts on paper. Drawings become visualisations, visualisations become decisions, and decisions become a finished room.

  1. Sketch

    Hand drawings fix the idea: proportion, flow and atmosphere, before anything is modelled.

  2. Visualisation

    We use modern tools, including AI and parametric design software, to show complex concepts before construction begins.

  3. Realisation

    Technical drawings, vendors, timeline and quality control are managed in one hand, through to the handover.
