Services

Interior design and turn-key build in Amsterdam.

Concept design, technical drawings and turn-key build, for hospitality, residential and commercial interiors. Hire one discipline or let us run the whole route.

03

Concept Design

Interior concept design is where a brand, a story or a way of living is translated into a room. We start from the identity of the client and work towards a space that is distinctive, coherent and buildable.

  • Brand identity integration
  • Colour palette & material selection
  • Custom furniture & fixture design
→ 01

Technical Drawings

A good concept needs drawings that contractors can build from. We document the technical side of the interior so that the design survives contact with the building site.

  • Electrical schematics & load planning
  • Air conditioning & ventilation installation
  • Comprehensive lighting plans
→ 02

Turn-key Build

Turn-key interior build means one responsible partner from the first drawing to handover. We coordinate the trades, protect the timeline and check the quality, so you receive a finished space.

  • Strategic outsourcing & vendor management
  • Project watchdogging & timeline control
  • Rigorous quality control & safety standards
