Executive Suite

Hospitality Concept Design

A suite that merges workspace functionality with hospitality comfort, with discrete work zones and a calm lounge.

The brief

Create an executive suite that merges workspace functionality with luxury hospitality.

The challenge

Business travellers needed a space that could move seamlessly from work to relaxation.

Our approach

Interior concept design is where a brand, a story or a way of living is translated into a room. We start from the identity of the client and work towards a space that is distinctive, coherent and buildable.

The outcome

Discrete work zones with premium ergonomics blend into a sophisticated living environment. The suite serves productivity without sacrificing the sense of retreat.
